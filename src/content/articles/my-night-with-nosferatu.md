The one positive thing I can say about our Nosferatu night at the Peckhamplex is that we weren't late. A rare state of affairs when Fergal is involved. The bus was crowded. I had a cold. The temperature was in the single figures. 

As for the film itself, a frankly excessive number of minutes are dedicated to watching Lily-Rose Depp stare out a window, taking breaths. I have no doubt the experience would be riveting for anyone who doesn't partake in both activities on a regular basis.

Fish maybe.

An hour in and if I have to see one more white nightgown blowing in the wind, or listen to a single other character bemoan "The Plague"—each time requiring a new scene and several more seconds off my rapidly shortening life—I might just fall asleep.

Instead, I consider what I will order at McDonalds after it is over. This occupies me for at least an hour. 

By this point Lily-Rose Depp has engaged in what I can only assume was supposed to be a character arc. Turns out **SPOILER** she has to have *sex* with the evil vampire to kill him (we look up who wrote the film afterwards. In a shocking turn of events, it turns out to be a man.)

All in all, if you like looking at *nipples* I would recommend this film.

If you like burgers I would skip the film and go somewhere other than McDonalds as, to be honest, it let me down too.

If you like to be entertained, buy Fergal and Daisy large Sprites at the start of the film, and watch them sprint down Peckham High Street in search of a bathroom afterwards.
